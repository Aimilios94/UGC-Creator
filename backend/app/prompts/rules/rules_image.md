# Image rules (pictures, Nano Banana)

One scene card is one picture. For a video ad it is the clip's first frame; for an image ad it is
the finished picture (a carousel has up to 5).

## Picture prompt order
One paragraph of 80 to 150 words, in this order:
1. subject and action (as a pose or a frozen moment): who or what is in frame, where the product
   is and how it is held, worn or placed;
2. setting and props: the place, time of day, background details;
3. composition: shot size (close-up, medium, wide), camera angle and height, lens feel (for
   example "26 mm phone lens" or "85 mm portrait"), where the product sits in the frame;
4. light: source, direction, softness, colour temperature;
5. colour palette and mood;
6. the visual style.

## Product fidelity
- With a product photo: keep the product exactly as in the photo: same shape, proportions,
  colours, logo and printed text.
- Without a product photo: draw the product from the written description only. Do not add
  logos or text that the description does not give.

## No text
Never ask for written words, captions, signs, labels or logos other than those printed on the
product. Headlines, the logo and the offer are added later, in editing.

## Character reference
- If the product photo shows a usable person (face visible), that photo is the reference for
  every picture of that person: same face, hair, skin and build.
- If not, and the same person appears in two or more scenes, one front-view picture of them is
  made first and used as the reference for every picture of them.
- Describe each person from their cast entry, the same way in every scene.

## Without a product photo
Pictures are made from text alone, from the same slots. The product line is the written
product description, not "as in the photo".
